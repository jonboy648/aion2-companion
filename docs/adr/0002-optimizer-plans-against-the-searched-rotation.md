# The build optimizer plans against the searched rotation, buys stigma ranks in blocks, and values buffs by rank

A comparison with the published community builds (`research/community_builds_vs_engine_2026-10-04.md`) showed the engine's own recommendation trailing the couga54 boss build in 8 of 8 classes. The causes were mechanical, so the fixes live in `app/aion2c/engine/`:

- **Specialties on every skill.** `choose_specs` compared each skill's best option with the running DPS instead of the DPS the loop started from, so only the first skill ever got options. It now also tries enabler + consumer pairs (a forced crit with a reset on crit) that are worth little alone.
- **Stigma ranks up to the real cap, bought as blocks.** The stigma pool stopped at rank 10 and bought one rank at a time, so a tier behind worthless ranks (stigma tiers open at 5/10/15/20) was invisible. A candidate is now the best DPS gain per point over the next rank or any block ending at a rank that unlocks a specialty option or slot. Caps are the real ones: skill points stop at 10 (Daevanion adds the rest), stigmas at the region stigma cap (20 in Global) or the skill's own rank table. A rank is valued with every specialty slot it has open, not the best single option. PROC skills the rotation casts now receive ranks.
- **Planning against the searched rotation.** Stigmas, ranks and specialties used to be scored against the greedy seed rotation, but casts gated on a status (`require_status`) exist only in searched rotations. When the searched rotation contains such a cast, the plan runs once more with that rotation leading the seed (`_heuristic_priority(..., guide=)`): the stigma search restarts from the first pass's picks and only swaps, the core skill ranks and (while the stigma set is unchanged) the Daevanion path are kept, stigma ranks, specialties and the rotation are redone, and the better of the two passes is kept. It is one extra pass, not "until stable", because every pass repeats the rotation search and the browser compare has to stay under twice its old time; when the searched rotation has no gated cast the seed was already a fair proxy and the second pass is skipped.
- **Rank-aware buffs.** The class data values 30 buff statuses at one anchor rank (its notes say "rank 20 used", "rank 16 used as the typical rank"). They now carry `Status.rank_scales` (class `mechanics.json` `rank_scales`, applied by `build_gamedata.apply_rank_scales`): the client anchor points quoted in those notes and the rank the data's own value is stated at. `engine/rank_values.py` re-values them in every simulation at the build's own rank of the skill that applies them (`total_rank`). `test_rank_scales_match_shipped_values` in `tests/test_optimizer_community.py` fails if a curve stops returning the shipped value at its stated rank. Statuses without a clear curve in their notes (for example `ee_robe_of_flame`, which follows the Robe of Flame passive) keep their data value. Consequence: a buff stigma left at rank 1 is now worth its rank-1 value, so DPS figures for builds with unspent or unset stigma ranks are lower than before and are not comparable with old numbers.
- **Stigmas that are not in Global** (no unlock level, no tiers) are never offered.
- **Stigma sets are scored at their planned rank with their tier options** (follow-up, same day). The set search scored every candidate at the build's own ranks with no specialty options, so a stigma whose value is its rank or a tier option (Spiritmaster Benediction and Corrode, Ranger Vaizel's Authority) looked worthless and the set that won was Magic Block, Jointstrike Destructive Attack and the like. A candidate is now scored with each stigma at the rank an equal share of the unspent stigma points buys (`_planned_stigma_rank`) and with the options of that rank chosen once per stigma (`tier_options`, `choose_specs(only=)`). The rank-aware set is compared with the plain set the old search would pick, by a short plan (ranks bought, Daevanion, specialties, a 60-candidate rotation search, each set also scored under the other's rotation: `_judge_sets`), and the better one is used; this guards classes where an equal share misjudges the real, concentrated purchase (Chanter AoE). A community rotation's stigmas for the scenario are tried as a set too. Only builds with stigma points to spend take this path: without points nothing is planned and the search is the one above.

## Assumption that is deliberately unchanged (superseded by ADR 0004: stigma tiers are automatic)

A stigma uses as many of its four tiers (options at rank 5/10/15/20) as core-skill mastery slots are open (`specs.active_options`: 1 of 1 at rank 5, 1 of 2 at 10, 2 of 3 at 15, 3 of 4 at 20). The client table note lists five stigma slot rows unlocked at 0 next to the three mastery slots, and the guides treat tiers as automatic, so the true rule may be "every unlocked tier is active"; that is not verified in game and the report measures it moving only the Cleric (+19% to +24%). It stays as is until someone confirms it in game. The pair search makes the Cleric's Earth Punishment tier 5 reachable under the current rule.
