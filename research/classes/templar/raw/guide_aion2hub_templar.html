AION 2 Templar

수호성

Longsword-and-shield guardian who locks enemy aggro and anchors the front line.

TankWeapon: LongswordElyos & Asmodians42 skills

The Templar is AION 2's dedicated tank, a heavily armored frontliner who fights with a longsword and shield. Built to absorb punishment and command attention, it uses taunts like Provoke to pin a boss's aggro to itself while shielding the party behind it. Its kit blends sustained threat from strikes such as Vicious Strike with damage-mitigation tools like Doom Shield and hard-hitting follow-ups in Punishment and Decisive Strike. The result is the anchor of dungeon groups and a durable line-holder in PvP.

Playstyle

A deliberate, control-focused tank: chain Vicious Strike to build and hold aggro, lock targets with Provoke, then time Doom Shield and damage-reduction abilities to survive boss burst while keeping allies safe. It rewards players who enjoy threat management and well-timed defensive cooldowns over raw output.

AION 2 Templar FAQ

  • What role is the AION 2 Templar?

    The Templar is a Tank class. A deliberate, control-focused tank: chain Vicious Strike to build and hold aggro, lock targets with Provoke, then time Doom Shield and damage-reduction abilities to survive boss burst while keeping allies safe. It rewards players who enjoy threat management and well-timed defensive cooldowns over raw output.

  • What weapon does the AION 2 Templar use?

    The Templar wields the Longsword.

  • Can both factions play the Templar?

    Yes — like every AION 2 class, the Templar is playable by both the Elyos and the Asmodians.

  • How many skills does the AION 2 Templar have?

    42 are listed on this page: 35 core skills from the Global client's skill list (25 active, 10 passive) and 7 more (7 active) from Korea/Taiwan data (client v110, 2026-09-09), not yet verified against the Global client.
