 AION 2 Templar — Main tank
 Templar is one of the eight launch classes in AION 2, filling the main tank role in the Warrior archetype. Its official class description reads: A sworn guardian who stands firm like a fortress wall, holding the battlefield with an unbreakable shield .
 Official description quoted from NCSOFT’s AION 2 class reveal. Source: aion2.plaync.com · Verified: 2026-09-28

 How the Templar Plays
 The Templar is the class you pick when you want to decide where a fight happens. Its whole kit points at one job: hold attention off everyone else. Community reports describe a rotation built around guard skills and taunts, with more than one way to close distance — pulling a mob to you, or charging through it.
 The trade-off is damage. It has fewer high-damage skills than any of the other seven, and its ranged attacks are short-range with long cooldowns, so poking from a distance is not a real option. Reported open-world solo damage is slow.
 Worth knowing before you commit: this is one of the first two classes NCSOFT chose to rework, so the version you play at global launch may differ from the one described in older KR/TW footage.

 AION 2 Templar FAQ
 Is the Templar the main tank in AION 2?
 Yes. The Templar is the dedicated tank class, built around holding aggro and mitigating damage. Gladiator can fill a tank slot in a pinch but is a bruiser rather than a dedicated tank.
 Can the Templar deal damage in AION 2?
 It deals meaningful damage — every AION 2 class does — but it has fewer high-damage skills than the other seven. Reported solo damage in open-world content is slow.
 Is the Templar good for solo play?
 It is survivable but slow. The class is built for group content; if soloing is your priority, Chanter or Cleric are reported as more comfortable.
