 Templar PvE Guide
 article 
 Contents 
 01 Overview
 The Templar is AION 2's dedicated shield tank. Unlike Gladiator (bruiser/DPS), Templar's primary job is: keep the boss focused on you while reducing incoming damage and protecting the party .
 September 2026 matter because Templar received a major rework on August 26 , then further PvE balance on September 2 and September 4 . Pre-September guides should not be copied blindly.
 Name note: Will to Survive → Will to Survive (Survival Willpower) . Threatening Blow is the Vicious Strike Spec 5 chain finisher (no standalone wiki page). Community “Single-Target Taunt” → Taunt . “AoE Taunt” and “Avoiding Strike” appear in KR community write-ups but have no dedicated EN wiki pages yet — use Taunt + pack tools like Grapple / Insulting Roar , and defensive stigmas such as Doom Shield / Second Skin / Nezekan's Shield as the closest documented options.
 A good Templar must: maintain Enmity, control boss facing, Block attacks, pre-cast defensives, manage MP, use Shield skills without killing uptime, and protect the party when needed. A weak DPS can be carried; a tank who loses the boss can wipe the group.
 Strengths 
 Excellent survivability (Block, Resistance, Tolerance, shields, defensives)
 Strong Enmity generation on core attacks
 Party protection utility beyond self-mitigation
 Tanking priority 
 Survivability > Enmity stability > DPS — do not build Templar like Gladiator.

 06 Stigma Build
 Templar Stigmas cover Enmity, mitigation, and emergency survival — not personal DPS.
 Stigma Priority Notes 
 Taunt S Single-target Taunt — first high-level investment 
 Shield of Protection S Frontal Block mitigation — standard dungeon defense 
 Punishment A / Core Keep Executor uptime in the build 
 Second Skin A +30% PvE Damage Tolerance (10s) at base; stronger at high levels 
 Nezekan's Shield Situational Strong shield (~60% Max HP reported at Lv.20 in KR tests) 
 Doom Shield A / Situational Closest documented defensive stigma to community “Avoiding Strike” 
 Insulting Roar Utility Enmity Boost self-buff — useful on pulls 
 Standard dungeon tank 
 S: Taunt , Shield of Protection 
 A: Punishment , Doom Shield / Second Skin 
 Hard bosses: swap in Second Skin or Nezekan's Shield 
 Community guides also list a separate “AoE Taunt” stigma. The current EN database only documents Taunt . For packs, combine Taunt with Grapple , Assault Fury , and Insulting Roar .
 07 Tank Rotation
 Templar uses a priority system , not a strict DPS script.
 1. Boss control — Taunt when needed
 2. Defensives — Warding Strike / Shield of Protection / Second Skin 
 3. Judgment 
 4. Punishment — maintain Executor
 5. Vicious Strike chain — damage, Enmity, Warding Strike CDR
 6. Shield Smite — MP + Judgment engine
 Single-boss opener 
 Taunt → Punishment → Vicious Strike chain → Judgment → Shield Smite → Pummel → Punishing Strike → Judgment → repeat
 Establish Enmity before DPS burst. If the boss opens with a killer attack, insert a defensive cooldown early — a dead tank has zero Enmity.
 AoE pull 
 AoE threat / Taunt → position pack → Punishment → Vicious Strike chain → Judgment → Shield Smite → AoE skills → re-Taunt as needed
 Keep mobs stacked for Sorcerer / Spiritmaster / Gladiator / Ranger AoE.
 Beginner rotation 
 Taunt → Punishment → Vicious Strike → Judgment → Shield Smite → Pummel → Punishing Strike → Judgment → repeat; Warding Strike for big hits; Defiance for CC
 Advanced (post–Sep 4 CDR loop) 
 Punishment → Vicious Strike chain → Judgment → Shield Smite → Judgment → Pummel / Punishing Strike (reduce Punishment) → Punishment → Vicious Strike chain (reduce Warding Strike) → defensive window → repeat
 Cancel style 
 Standard KR configuration: Vicious Strike chain → Judgment cancel . Learn this first. A Pummel-heavy sustain variant exists but was considered weaker for max performance in August testing; re-test after September chain buffs.
 Defensive layers — do not stack all at once 
 Normal damage: Shield of Protection 
 Strong boss attack: Warding Strike 
 Extreme mechanic: Second Skin / Nezekan's Shield 
 CC mechanic: Defiance 
 Boss positioning 
 Boss faces Templar. Party stays behind / outside frontal cones. Avoid spinning the boss through the party. Use mobile Shield Smite and Punishment Spec 4 to adjust without stopping the rotation.
 MP management 
 KR testing reported cancel rotations can starve MP after ~3 minutes. Shield Smite Spec 2 (no cost + 200 MP on hit) is critical. Also use consumables, cut filler, and don't dump skills into dying adds.
 Combat Speed 
 A KR endgame test suggested ~ 126%+ Combat Speed as a practical Vicious Strike chain benchmark with a 15% battlefield buff. Treat as a testing benchmark, not an official hard cap.
 08 Stats, Stones, Arcana & Gear
 Tier Stats 
 Primary tank Block · Damage Resistance · Damage Tolerance · Max HP · Enmity 
 Offensive secondary Attack · Weapon Damage · Damage Amplification · Accuracy · Critical Hit 
 Block is a core defensive stat — not optional. HP matters for EHP and HP-scaling shields, but do not pure-stack HP over Block/Resistance. Damage Tolerance helps unblockable mechanics (see Second Skin ). Accuracy supports reliable Enmity once defenses are met.
 Magic Stones (main tank) 
 Block
 → HP
 → Damage Resistance
 → Accuracy
 → Attack / Damage
 Arcana: Block, Damage Resistance, HP, Damage Tolerance, Enmity, Accuracy first. Offensive Arcana only after survival is comfortable.
 Weapon + Shield 
 Longsword: Weapon Damage → Attack → Accuracy → Damage Boost → defensive options
 Shield: Block → defensive stats → HP → Damage Resistance → tank bonuses
 Gear progression 
 Early: Weapon + Shield enhancement → Block → HP → Accuracy
 Mid: Block + Damage Resistance + HP + Tolerance + Accuracy
 End: balanced Block / Resistance / Tolerance / HP with enough Accuracy + Attack for threat

 10 Common Mistakes & Playstyles
 Too much DPS gear — Templar is a tank first
 Taunt too late — don't wait until DPS already has aggro
 Spinning the boss — party should not chase
 Stacking all defensives — layer cooldowns
 Ignoring MP — rotation can starve
 Ignoring Shield Smite — it is your MP/Judgment engine
 Never using Warding Strike — unused defensives = zero mitigation
 Tank vs DPS Templar 
 Dungeon / hard PvE: full tank build
 Solo / farm: more offensive or hybrid is fine

 13 TL;DR
 Core systems: Judgment + Vicious Strike chain + Shield Smite, with Punishment/Executor windows.
 Defense: Warding Strike + Shield of Protection + Second Skin / Nezekan's Shield .
 Real DPS metric: boss uptime + Enmity uptime + party survival — not personal parse.
 September 4: Vicious Strike reduces Warding Strike; Pummel/Punishing Strike reduces Punishment — the rotation is deliberately interconnected.
