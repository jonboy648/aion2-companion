01
Overview
The 
Spiritmaster
 (wiki class: 
Elementalist
) is one of the most distinctive PvE DPS classes in AION 2. Damage comes from direct spells, elemental spirits, joint attacks, DoTs/debuffs, and 
Elemental Fusion
 — not from burst casting alone.
Endgame PvE revolves around spirit uptime, keeping debuffs active, treating 
Flame Incineration (Combustion)
 as the main filler, and spending 
Elemental Fusion
 on real damage windows.
Name note: 
Community guides often use alternate EN names. This article links to wiki localization — e.g. Flame Incineration → 
Combustion
, Frost Shock → 
Cold Shock
, Spatial Domination → 
Dimensional Control
, Concentration → 
Mental Focus
, Spirit's Grace → 
Enhance: Spirit's Benediction
, Fixed Spirit → 
Command: Proxy
.
Strengths
Excellent sustained PvE damage
Strong contribution from spirits
Good debuff utility and effect uptime
Strong on long-lived bosses
Spirits can keep hitting during some player mechanics
Scales well from skill levels and offensive stats
Weaknesses
More complicated than it first appears
Poor prioritization noticeably cuts DPS
Spirit management matters
Much damage comes from maintaining conditions, not single burst presses
Movement/mechanics can reduce spirit uptime

03
Spirit Skills
Elemental summons are a major part of the class. September client changes set spirit cast time to 0 (instant), reducing animation loss.
Spirit
PvE notes
Summon: Fire Spirit
Primarily offensive damage setup
Summon: Water Spirit
High value in standard rotation / debuff interaction — prioritize after core DPS
Summon: Earth Spirit
Control/enmity utility; usually below pure DPS investment
Summon: Wind Spirit
Elemental system / spirit rotation piece
Summon: Ancient Spirit
Endgame summon; can coexist with the four elements (April change)
Spirit uptime = DPS. 
Resummon on timing after boss movement; do not sacrifice major damage windows for low-value summons.

07
PvE Rotation
Spiritmaster is not a rigid seven-button macro class. Split play into opener, spirit setup, debuff maintenance, Fusion, and filler.
Opener (practical)
Flame Blessing
Basic attacks
Spirit's Grace (Enhance: Spirit's Benediction)
Fixed Spirit (Command: Proxy)
Apply debuffs
Elemental Fusion
 (on a real window)
Main DPS loop
Frost Shock (Cold Shock)
Jointstrike: Curse
Flame Incineration (Combustion)
Spatial Domination (Dimensional Control)
Elemental Fusion
 when ready
Practical priority
Keep important debuffs active
Maintain spirit setup
Use Elemental Fusion when available (good windows)
Spam Combustion as primary filler
Fill downtime with secondary attacks
Macro tip: 
Macro only 
Flame Incineration (Combustion)
. Keep Frost Shock / Fusion / Curse / Dimensional Control / Flame Blessing / Spirit's Grace / Fixed Spirit on manual keys so cooldowns are not wasted.
Fixed Spirit downtime: 
when Proxy/spirit setup drops, use spirit skills ×3 → 
Summon: Water Spirit
 with ~1s spacing before Water Spirit to avoid animation cancel loss.

Accuracy first when needed: 
if you miss content caps, Accuracy > extra Critical Hit until the threshold is met. Do not blindly stack crit — the kit already supplies a lot of offensive efficiency.
Magic stones
Weapon Damage Increase is especially efficient because 
Flame Blessing
, 
Jointstrike: Curse
,

, and 
Spirit's Grace (Enhance: Spirit's Benediction)
 already amplify other layers.
Weapon Damage Increase
Damage Increase
Critical Damage Increase
Attack Power
Critical Hit / Accuracy when needed
Arcana: 
prefer offensive passives — Damage Amp, Crit Damage, Weapon Damage, Impact, Attack Power, spirit-related offense. Chalice/Scales-style collections of useful passives beat flashy but low-synergy setups.
Gear
Weapon: 
Weapon Damage, Damage Amp, Attack Power, Accuracy, Crit Damage
Armor: 
Damage Amp, Crit Damage Increase, Perfect, Attack Power
Accessories: 
Attack Power, Crit, Accuracy, Damage Amp, skill bonuses where applicable
Keep enough Damage Resistance / HP / Impact Resist / status resist for the encounter, then push offense.
09
Daevanion
Eight class boards (Nezekan → Yustiel). For Spiritmaster PvE:
1. 
Skill-level nodes for main PvE abilities
2. 
Attack / Attack Bonus
3. 
Cooldown Reduction
4. 
Critical Hit
5. 
Max MP / defensive stats
Skill node priority: 
Flame Incineration (Combustion)
, 
Elemental Fusion
, 
Spirit Strike
, major spirit skills, then Attack / Damage Amp / Crit / CDR.
Take the strongest offensive route, not the shortest path. Exact node coordinates change with board data — verify on the current planner.
10
Boss Encounters
Excellent situations
Long single-target fights
Stationary bosses
Predictable mechanics
DoTs that stay active
Spirits that can keep attacking
Harder situations
Constant target switching
Long invulnerability phases
Frequent forced movement
Mechanics that repeatedly break spirit uptime
Execution gap is large on this class: uptime on spirits, debuffs, Combustion, and Fusion windows matters more than tooltip burst.
