Spiritmaster Daevanion Boards

The game calls this system Daevanion; players carry the name Stigma over from the original Aion. A Spiritmaster opens 5 boards, each a 15x15 grid that grows from the centre, and a node costs 1 to 4 points with the start of each board free.

The points come from separate pools, so what a board costs only matters against its own: DaevanionCrystal asks 570 of 840 and BattleCrystal asks 232 of 232. Every board on this page fits its pool whole, so the choice here is the order you take the nodes in, not which ones you give up.
