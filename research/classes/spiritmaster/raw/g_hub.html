AION 2 Spiritmaster

정령성

Orb-wielding spirit summoner who wears enemies down with elemental damage, fear, and turret-like spirits.

DPSWeapon: OrbElyos & Asmodians79 skills

The Spiritmaster (internal id Elementalist) is one of AION 2's eight classes — a ranged DPS who channels the four elements through an Orb. Rather than trading blows up close, it summons fire, water, wind, and earth spirits that act as stationary, turret-like helpers while the caster layers damage-over-time, debuffs, and fear on enemies from a distance. Signature skills such as Cold Shock, Earth Tremor, and Combustion deliver its elemental damage, Siphon drains life for sustain, and Soul's Cry and Ashy Call anchor its crowd control. It is widely regarded as one of the strongest solo classes, with high-impact PvP control, at the cost of a demanding rotation.

Playstyle

A control-oriented ranged caster: deploy and rotate elemental spirits for sustained pressure, stack elemental DoTs and debuffs, and use fear-based AoE in Soul's Cry and Ashy Call plus Siphon's lifesteal to survive. Its damage falls off sharply without active summons, so it rewards constant spirit upkeep — potent solo and in PvP, but more hands-on in group play.

AION 2 Spiritmaster FAQ

  • What role is the AION 2 Spiritmaster?

    The Spiritmaster is a DPS class. A control-oriented ranged caster: deploy and rotate elemental spirits for sustained pressure, stack elemental DoTs and debuffs, and use fear-based AoE in Soul's Cry and Ashy Call plus Siphon's lifesteal to survive. Its damage falls off sharply without active summons, so it rewards constant spirit upkeep — potent solo and in PvP, but more hands-on in group play.

  • What weapon does the AION 2 Spiritmaster use?

    The Spiritmaster wields the Orb.

  • Can both factions play the Spiritmaster?

    Yes — like every AION 2 class, the Spiritmaster is playable by both the Elyos and the Asmodians.
