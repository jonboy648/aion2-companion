Spiritmaster PvE Guide

article

01Overview

The Spiritmaster (wiki class: Elementalist) is one of the most distinctive PvE DPS classes in AION 2. Damage comes from direct spells, elemental spirits, joint attacks, DoTs/debuffs, and Elemental Fusion — not from burst casting alone.

Endgame PvE revolves around spirit uptime, keeping debuffs active, treating Flame Incineration (Combustion) as the main filler, and spending Elemental Fusion on real damage windows.
Name note: Community guides often use alternate EN names. This article links to wiki localization — e.g. Flame Incineration → Combustion, Frost Shock → Cold Shock, Spatial Domination → Dimensional Control, Concentration → Mental Focus, Spirit's Grace → Enhance: Spirit's Benediction, Fixed Spirit → Command: Proxy.

Strengths

  • Excellent sustained PvE damage
  • Strong contribution from spirits
  • Good debuff utility and effect uptime
  • Strong on long-lived bosses
  • Spirits can keep hitting during some player mechanics
  • Scales well from skill levels and offensive stats

Weaknesses

  • More complicated than it first appears
  • Poor prioritization noticeably cuts DPS
  • Spirit management matters
  • Much damage comes from maintaining conditions, not single burst presses
  • Movement/mechanics can reduce spirit uptime

02Core PvE Skills

These skills form the foundation of the current PvE setup.

Flame Incineration (Combustion)Main DPSHighest early investment — push toward Lv.20+

Elemental Fusion loop: build elemental state → four charges → activate Fusion → spend the state → return to spirit/debuff rotation. Do not dump Fusion into invulnerability windows.

03Spirit Skills

Elemental summons are a major part of the class. September client changes set spirit cast time to 0 (instant), reducing animation loss.

Summon: Water SpiritHigh value in standard rotation / debuff interaction — prioritize after core DPS
Summon: Earth SpiritControl/enmity utility; usually below pure DPS investment

Spirit uptime = DPS. Resummon on timing after boss movement; do not sacrifice major damage windows for low-value summons.

05Recommended Skill Leveling Order

  1. Flame Incineration (Combustion) → Lv.20+
  2. Spirit Strike
  3. Concentration (Mental Focus)
  4. Frost Shock (Cold Shock)
  5. Jointstrike: Curse
  6. Elemental Fusion
  7. Spirit Revival (Spirit Revitalization)
  8. Summon: Water Spirit
  9. Other spirit skills
  10. Corrosion (Corrode) and lower-value options
Concentrate investment into roughly four main actives instead of leveling the entire list evenly.

07PvE Rotation

Spiritmaster is not a rigid seven-button macro class. Split play into opener, spirit setup, debuff maintenance, Fusion, and filler.

Opener (practical)

  1. Flame Blessing
  2. Basic attacks
  3. Spirit's Grace (Enhance: Spirit's Benediction)
  4. Fixed Spirit (Command: Proxy)
  5. Apply debuffs
  6. Elemental Fusion (on a real window)

Main DPS loop

  1. Frost Shock (Cold Shock)
  2. Jointstrike: Curse
  3. Flame Incineration (Combustion)
  4. Spatial Domination (Dimensional Control)
  5. Elemental Fusion when ready

Practical priority

  1. Keep important debuffs active
  2. Maintain spirit setup
  3. Use Elemental Fusion when available (good windows)
  4. Spam Combustion as primary filler
  5. Fill downtime with secondary attacks
Macro tip: Macro only Flame Incineration (Combustion). Keep Frost Shock / Fusion / Curse / Dimensional Control / Flame Blessing / Spirit's Grace / Fixed Spirit on manual keys so cooldowns are not wasted.

Fixed Spirit downtime: when Proxy/spirit setup drops, use spirit skills ×3 → Summon: Water Spirit with ~1s spacing before Water Spirit to avoid animation cancel loss.

08Stats, Stones, Arcana & Gear

General offensive priority

  1. Damage Amplification
  2. Critical Damage Increase
  3. Weapon Damage Increase
  4. Impact
  5. Attack Power
  6. Perfect
  7. Critical Hit / Accuracy
Accuracy first when needed: if you miss content caps, Accuracy > extra Critical Hit until the threshold is met. Do not blindly stack crit — the kit already supplies a lot of offensive efficiency.

Magic stones

Weapon Damage Increase is especially efficient because Flame Blessing, Jointstrike: Curse, Corrosion (Corrode), and Spirit's Grace (Enhance: Spirit's Benediction) already amplify other layers.

  1. Weapon Damage Increase
  2. Damage Increase
  3. Critical Damage Increase
  4. Attack Power
  5. Critical Hit / Accuracy when needed

Arcana: prefer offensive passives — Damage Amp, Crit Damage, Weapon Damage, Impact, Attack Power, spirit-related offense. Chalice/Scales-style collections of useful passives beat flashy but low-synergy setups.

Gear

  • Weapon: Weapon Damage, Damage Amp, Attack Power, Accuracy, Crit Damage
  • Armor: Damage Amp, Crit Damage Increase, Perfect, Attack Power
  • Accessories: Attack Power, Crit, Accuracy, Damage Amp, skill bonuses where applicable

Keep enough Damage Resistance / HP / Impact Resist / status resist for the encounter, then push offense.

09Daevanion

Eight class boards (Nezekan → Yustiel). For Spiritmaster PvE:

  1. 1. Skill-level nodes for main PvE abilities
  2. 2. Attack / Attack Bonus
  3. 3. Cooldown Reduction
  4. 4. Critical Hit
  5. 5. Max MP / defensive stats

Skill node priority: Flame Incineration (Combustion), Elemental Fusion, Spirit Strike, major spirit skills, then Attack / Damage Amp / Crit / CDR.

Take the strongest offensive route, not the shortest path. Exact node coordinates change with board data — verify on the current planner.

10Boss Encounters

Excellent situations

  • Long single-target fights
  • Stationary bosses
  • Predictable mechanics
  • DoTs that stay active
  • Spirits that can keep attacking

Harder situations

  • Constant target switching
  • Long invulnerability phases
  • Frequent forced movement
  • Mechanics that repeatedly break spirit uptime
Execution gap is large on this class: uptime on spirits, debuffs, Combustion, and Fusion windows matters more than tooltip burst.

13TL;DR

Skills: Flame Incineration (Combustion) → Spirit Strike → Concentration (Mental Focus) → Frost Shock (Cold Shock) / Jointstrike: Curse / Elemental Fusion → Spirit Revival (Spirit Revitalization) → Summon: Water Spirit.

Stigma: Fixed Spirit (Command: Proxy) + Spirit's Grace (Enhance: Spirit's Benediction) + Corrosion (Corrode), then Flame Blessing / Shadow of Curse (Cursed Cloud).

Play: maintain spirits and debuffs, spam Combustion, spend Fusion on real boss windows.
