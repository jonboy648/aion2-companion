# Sorcerer combos: damage, stagger, and native setup

2026-10-24 — research only. **Yes: reuse our build/rotation engine and native setup editor to recommend combos. Damage recommendations are model-based today; best stagger combos and optimized native execution require additional modeling and observed game evidence.** No universal winning sequence follows from skill tables alone.

Sources: this worktree's [CONTEXT](../CONTEXT.md), [ADR0001](../docs/adr/0001-game-numbers-from-private-client-export.md), shipped derived data, and read-only private Tables. Private locators below mean `AION2_EXPORT_DIR/<table>.json`, `Properties.Data`, keyed by `ID.Value` unless specified otherwise. The historical export is from Oct 4; its patch is unknown. It is **not identified with today's installed build**. Only derived facts are recorded here.

## Content and objective are separate

CONTEXT defines five content playstyles: Boss, Timed boss, Leveling, AoE horde, PvP. The checked-out [engine registry](../app/aion2c/engine/build_optimizer.py) (`PLAYSTYLES`) currently implements Boss = 180 s/one boss, AoE = 30 s/four targets, Leveling = 15 s/three targets, and a 15 s/one-boss burst opener. Timed boss and PvP are not implemented there; `optimize_full_build` explicitly warns that PvP is not modeled. Do not relabel its burst opener as a complete PvP model.

For each supported content scenario, choose an objective separately: maximize modeled HP damage over its horizon; maximize gauge contribution within a break window; or maximize HP damage subject to a stagger requirement. A weighted compromise needs an explicit tradeoff. At a fixed horizon, maximizing total HP damage and DPS gives the same ranking. [Scenario/SimResult contracts](../app/aion2c/models.py) contain duration, targets, boss flag and HP results, but no objective, stagger threshold, immunity profile or gauge state. [Priority search](../app/aion2c/engine/search.py) (`optimize`) and full-build selection score `.dps`; seeded local search is budget-limited, not proof of a global optimum.

Two further limits matter when interpreting damage rankings:

- The generic engine supports cast/crit/status triggers, conditional windows and free procs, but shipped Sorcerer `triggers` contains only two Fire Mark entries, both at 20% per fire cast. Private `SkillAbnormalEffect[1571000011/1571000012]` separates guaranteed Fire Mark application from its 20% damage trigger (`Values[0]` helper skill, `[1]` chance), restricts attacks to Fire and Normal/Critical (`[5]/[6]`), and carries the interval token (`[2]`) used as 1 s in `Entries.SkillString_STR_SKILL_PC_SORCERER_15710000_skill_desc_effect`. Shipped direct `rules.*.applies` supplies the guaranteed mark for several Fire skills, but this is not a complete import of those trigger restrictions/intervals. Generic trigger capability does not imply every Sorcerer passive is connected.
- Stagger-dependent Flame Scattershot `15010000` has primary `SkillCond[15000002].CondAbnormalEffectType=Groggy`. Gamedata tags it `needs_stagger`; `_resolve` requires `staggered`, and search excludes it when nothing applies that status. Sorcerer defines no such status/window. There is no threshold, depletion, recovery, break duration or staggered damage bonus simulation. HP estimates also retain fitted defense/crit/boss factors in `damage.py`, uncertain animation locks, and unquantified specialties.

The wiring passes through [specparse.finalize_gamedata](../app/aion2c/specparse.py), [mechanics.json.specialization_effects](../app/aion2c/data/src/mechanics.json), [specs.active_options](../app/aion2c/specs.py) and simulator `_build_specs`. Parsing a specialty into an effect is distinct from faithfully reproducing its target conditions and event timing.

## What can be computed now

**Damage:** given a legal build, stats and supported scenario, reuse `simulate`, `search.optimize`, `optimize_full_build` and the existing cast explanation. They already connect ranks, specialty choices, cooldown changes/resets, MP, buffs/DoTs and selected charge levels. Flame Arrow `15210000` → Burst `15030000` → Pyroclasm `15250000` is explicitly connected by `rules.*.chain_next`; Winter's Illusion is instead approximated as extra damage/lock/cost on Winter's Shackles specialty index 3, not a separate chain event. These are specific modeled connections, not a promise of complete class mechanics.

**Stagger:** expose the verified effect amounts and compare provisional contributions for a specified window. First preserve charge-dependent amounts and establish effect multiplicity; do not sum the current Hellfire scalar for every release or multiply all gauge values by `hits`. Actual break time requires a target's threshold and recovery rules.

**Native setup:** reuse [nativeConfig](../web/src/features/keybinds/nativeConfig.ts) (`NativeConfig`, `resolveNativeColumn`, placement/binding checks), [nativeSetup](../web/src/features/keybinds/nativeSetup.ts), and existing storage/editor. They support twelve stable Quick Use identities, four authored rows, one activation binding, named recipes, column-linked entries and independent 10–9900 ms delays. `PCContextSkillSlot` Sorcerer slot 1 fixes Flame Arrow `15210000`; `Skill[15210000].ContextSkillSlotRegisterType=FixSlot`. The private [Quick Use derivation](../app/aion2c/data/build_quick_use.py) exactly reproduced shipped `client_quick_use.json` in this inspection.

Row 0 is highest priority: an always-eligible filler there can starve rows 1–3. Primary help keys are `String_UI_SKILL_CONTEXT_INFO_GUIDE_body` and `String_UI_SKILL_SETTING_MACRO_DESC_body` in `L10N/en-US/L10NString.json`; they establish eligibility selection, held activation, manual precedence and non-guaranteed execution order. `Message_MSG_SKILL_SETTING_MACRO_DELAYLIMIT_body` and `GlobalSetting.skill_macro_delay_min/max` establish the delay range. They **do not establish retries, queue capacity, delay placement, column exhaustion, chain substitution or charge release under a macro**. The editor's linked-column representation remains a configuration model, not proof of the game's persisted target behavior.

[Prior native research](native-macro-rules-2026-10-11.md#macro-set-row-list-and-icon-values) withdrew the 20-step claim: `GlobalSetting.max_skill_macro_set/max_skill_macro_row/max_skill_macro_Icon/skill_macro_list_max` are distinct constants with unverified consumers. Website storage limits are not native capacity. Legacy [macro.py](../app/aion2c/keybinds/macro.py), `simulator.run_macro` and the older [quick_use.native_plan](../app/aion2c/keybinds/quick_use.py) retain assumptions including wrapping order, manual-only charge policy or 20 entries. Do not reuse those as verified execution rules. [webapi.keybinds](../app/aion2c/webapi.py) calls the native setup path and returns `queue_status=unverified`, without simulating native DPS; preserve that boundary. See also the [shipped integration verification](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-native-macro-verification.md).

## Minimal follow-up and public test boundaries

1. Extend the existing derivation with only allowlisted numbers/conditions: stable IDs, per-release gauge, alternate charge thresholds, conditional reset/proc metadata and source confidence. Keep the private root environmental, raw exports excluded, and drift checks from ADR0001. Reuse rank/spec contracts; do not infer purchased ranks from imported effective ranks.
2. Add an objective/scorer beside the existing scenario, pass it through priority **and** stigma/rank/specialty/Daevanion selection, and add gauge results separately from HP damage. Until target evidence exists, label gauge scores as proxies. Retain search budgets and uncertainty warnings.
3. Seed suggestions into the current native editor, validate placements, and export an editable setup sheet. Keep authored delays and charge warnings; offer conditional column placement reasoning without claiming optimal native timing. A UI rebuild is unnecessary.
4. Use small, public synthetic fixtures: literal per-tier gauge/hit values; unlocked versus locked specialties; Firestorm/Hellfire cooldown interactions; successful versus failed Frost application; immunity route present versus absent; deterministic proc limitations; and horizon clipping. One micro-probe found that a maximum Hellfire started in a **0.1 s** scenario still contributes HP damage despite a **1.5 s** release threshold (`run_priority` checks start time, `_cast` adds damage without rejecting a post-horizon release). Fix this model boundary before deadline-sensitive optimization. Native tests should cover row starvation/dependency, fixed Flame Arrow, stable identities after rebinding, acquisition/stigma gates, repeated configured columns and independent delay round trips—configuration behavior only.

Observed game evidence is specifically needed for current region/patch identity; target gauge threshold, reset/decay and break window; per-target/per-projectile gauge and trigger multiplicity; immune-target proc eligibility; hit/crit/internal-cooldown behavior; charge timing, animation recovery and interrupts; and native hold/release, retry/queue/replacement behavior with Skill Queue on/off and manual input. Follow the [existing capture protocol](quick-use-ingame-validation-2026-10-06.md), with visible settings and repeated discriminating cases. A combat damage total or UI table alone cannot establish the scheduler.

Validation performed: structured JSON/table joins, semantic comparison of shipped Sorcerer datasets, exact Quick Use re-derivation in memory, and short direct simulator probes.
